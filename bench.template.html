What this bench does. Drop both source documents as PDF or Word. The bench reads the text out of them in your browser and packages everything as one briefing packet that produces the headline and the body text — the parts that have to be written. Boilerplate, contacts and the dateline are added later in your own template.

It does not write the release itself — the drafting step stays in Claude, where the tdk-press-release skill applies the same rules encoded here. Everything on this page runs offline in your browser: no upload, no network, no account.
